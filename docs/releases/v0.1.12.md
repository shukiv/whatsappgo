# WhatsAppGo v0.1.12

Eighteen commits since v0.1.11. The app can now remove itself, hold a message
until a chosen moment, read a channel you follow, and move through a voice
note. A freshly linked account stops inventing unread badges and stops showing
one person as two conversations. It does not imply full WhatsApp feature
parity.

## Highlights

- The app removes itself, and everything it put on this computer, on request.
- A message can be written now and sent at a chosen moment.
- A channel you follow opens and reads its posts; a community you are in
  through one of its groups is listed at last.
- Voice notes can be moved through, and show when they were listened to.
- A newly linked account no longer shows unread badges on conversations the
  phone has already read, nor one person as two conversations.
- Message notifications stop going missing during a busy spell.

## Added

- **Uninstall:** `--uninstall` lists every path the program created with its
  size, waits for the word yes, and says what happened. `--yes` answers for a
  script; `--keep-accounts` leaves the accounts and their history. It refuses
  while a window is open, because the background service holds those databases.
  Two things it says rather than hides: removing the history cannot be undone,
  and WhatsApp is not told - the phone goes on listing this computer under
  Linked devices until somebody removes it there.
- **Scheduled messages:** the clock beside the send button holds a message
  until a moment you choose, shown in full rather than as two fields. What is
  waiting sits above the composer and can be cancelled. The honest limit is in
  the dialog: this only works while WhatsAppGo is running, and a message whose
  moment passes with the app closed goes out at the next start.
- **Channels and communities:** clicking a channel opens it and asks for its
  posts, which are then kept like any other message. There is no composer,
  because only the people who run a channel may post in one. A community you
  are in through one of its groups is now listed, which is how almost everybody
  is in a community.
- **Voice notes:** a handle shows where playback has reached. Drag it to move
  through a recording; letting go plays from there. The handle is green while a
  recording is waiting to be heard and blue once it has been played, whoever
  played it, and the chat list shows a green microphone beside a conversation
  holding one nobody has listened to.
- **Opening a conversation from a group:** clicking somebody's name above their
  message in a group opens a conversation with them, which clicking a mention
  of them already did.
- **Desktop identity:** the dock and the applications menu show the WhatsAppGo
  icon and name. A downloaded AppImage installs no launcher entry, so it writes
  one for itself on startup, and `--uninstall` takes it away again.
- **Library logging:** `WHATSAPPGO_LOG_LEVEL` takes DEBUG, INFO, WARN or ERROR
  and hands the WhatsApp library a logger. Anything else is refused by name, so
  a misspelling is not silence. It is off by default, and not only for the
  volume: at DEBUG the library writes the contents of every message it handles.

## Fixed

- **Unread badges on a new account:** history sync says how many messages of
  each conversation the phone still counts as unread, and that number was
  thrown away. Everything since the last read receipt this device happened to
  witness was counted as new - seventy-four here, ninety-nine there, on
  conversations the phone shows as finished. Nothing is sent to WhatsApp: this
  only writes down what the phone already said.
- **One person as two conversations:** WhatsApp addresses a person two ways,
  and whichever form arrived first opened a conversation keyed by it. Messages
  name the other address themselves, so the two are joined as a message arrives
  rather than only when the contact directory is fetched.
- **Account changes made while the app was closed:** call history and settings
  live in collections the server only notifies a connected device about, so
  every call taken with the app closed was never collected. Worse, a collection
  whose patches stopped verifying stayed frozen at the version it drifted on.
  Every collection is now asked for what it missed on each connection, and one
  that no longer verifies is replaced, or a fresh copy asked of the phone at
  most once a day.
- **Corrections made on another device:** an edit usually arrives wrapped in
  `editedMessage`, and only that shape was recognised. A copy of an edit made on
  another of your own devices can arrive as the bare protocol envelope, and a
  newsletter carries the new text directly - in both, the stanza's own edit
  attribute is what says so, and nothing read it. Missed, the correction was
  stored as an empty message nobody could see while the original kept its first
  wording.
- **Attachments the library refused to fetch:** an attachment carrying its key
  but not the hash of its encrypted bytes was read as unencrypted media, and
  the key thrown away, so it could never open. It is now fetched in two steps
  that learn that hash without reimplementing any of the cryptography. A
  message also names its attachment twice - a url and a direct path - and only
  one was ever read; the other is now tried before the phone is troubled.
- **A sweep that hammered the phone:** the background media collector walks the
  whole history and starts over when it finishes, so an attachment WhatsApp no
  longer serves came round on every pass. Each one asked the phone to send it
  again: 7717 requests in eight hours, 1716 of them for a single conversation.
  The sweep now asks for nothing, and an attachment somebody opens still asks
  once, which is what the media retry is for.
- **Notifications going missing:** the bound on how many notifications this
  daemon leaves open was applied after posting, and messages are delivered on a
  goroutine each, so a burst posted everything before anything was closed. The
  server's queue - shared with every other program in the session, and with a
  second account's daemon - filled, and once full it refuses everything: 140
  messages arrived over a day and a night without ever being shown. Room is now
  made before posting, and the two are one action.
- **A warning across the conversation:** scrolling asks the daemon for older
  history on its own, and the daemon declining a page it is already fetching
  was shown to the reader as a red failure. It is coordination, not a fault,
  and nobody asked for the page.
- **Voice note details:** a recording dragged to a point plays from that point
  rather than from the beginning, a flick that starts on a waveform scrolls the
  conversation instead of grabbing the recording, and a recording you sent is
  marked as listened to whoever listened.
- **Channel posts:** a post arriving while the channel is open is no longer
  acknowledged with a read receipt. Nobody is waiting to know that a follower
  read one, and the address takes no receipt.

## Known issues

- An edit made on the user's phone was reported as never appearing. The three
  shapes a correction can take are now all recognised, but that particular
  message left no trace of any kind in the stored history, so the event does
  not appear to have reached this computer at all. The library logging added in
  this release is what will answer it; it is tracked as `whatsappgo-45a`.
- The notification repair has not yet been watched over a long enough run to
  confirm the refusals have stopped. They appeared between one and forty times
  an hour, so only a day of use will say.
- `desktop-resize-rendering` aborts at teardown with a `QFontDatabase`
  ordering message. It is a race, not a regression, tracked as
  `whatsappgo-8hd`.

## Installation

Download the artifact for your platform and verify it against `SHA256SUMS`
before installation. Builds are unsigned: Windows SmartScreen may warn, and
macOS may require approval in System Settings. The release remains a draft
until the packaged binaries have been reviewed.
